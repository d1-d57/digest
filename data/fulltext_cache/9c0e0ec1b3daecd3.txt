Gowers, Group and semigroup puzzles and a possible Polymath project. Word problem for Artin-Tits groups (generalizing free/abelian/braid groups). Started from Tseytin semigroup (undecidable, unsuitable as playground) moved to Artin-Tits groups of unknown decidability. Interactive puzzle game with 'color-pushing' conjugation moves. Proposes iterative Polymath cycle: algorithm -> find failing puzzle -> improve -> attempt proof. Current algorithm solves all puzzles including Dehornoy's braid example.
