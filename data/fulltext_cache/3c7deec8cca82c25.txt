Joy of Why podcast with David Kipping (Columbia) on SETI/astrobiology. Reformulates Drake equation as birth-death process of civilizations: universe either crowded or empty, few intermediate scenarios. Discusses Fermi paradox, false biosignature claims (ALH84001, Martian canals, K2-18b dimethyl sulfide), proposes A/B testing methodology comparing planet populations instead of single biosignature searches. Exomoon research at Cool Worlds Lab.
