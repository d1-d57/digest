Gil Kalai's blog post announces three upcoming mathematical events in Israel, July 2025:

1. Annual Meeting of the Israeli Mathematical Union (July 6, Bar-Ilan University) — speakers Elon
Lindenstrauss and Amnon Shashua, plus 2025 Erdos Prize recipients Or Hershkovits and Eliran Subag;
ten parallel sessions. Followed by IMU student talks day (July 7) with plenary speakers Noga Alon
(Wolf Prize) and Adi Shamir, Nesiyahu prize recipient Pazit Haim-Kislev, and 31 graduate student
talks.

2. Joram Seminar (July 9-10) on "Hypercontractivity and Groups" — speakers Noam Lifshitz, Guy
Kindler (Hebrew University), Nathan Keller (Bar Ilan), Dor Minzer (MIT).

3. Amitsur Memorial Symposium (July 14), honoring Ilya (Eliyahu) Rips in memoriam.
