   A class of vertex algebra which has been extensively investigated in physical literature is
that of superconformal algebras, which consists of the Virasoro, Neveu-Schwarz, N = 2, 3, 4
and big N = 4 algebras. The study of their unitary representations has been a hot topic
in the late 80’s (see [26, 8, 9, 10, 11] and references therein). The superconformal algebras
were later realized as (an extension of) a special case of a relevant class of vertex algebras,
the minimal simple W -algebras Wkmin (g), associated to a basic simple finite-dimensional Lie
superalgebra g, its “minimal” sl2 -subalgebra s, and the level k ∈ C ([19, 21]). In particular,
the big N = 4 algebras is obtained from Wmin    k (D(2, 1; a)) by adding four fermions and one

boson (cf. (8.8)).
   In the paper [15] we began to study in a systematic way the unitarity of the VOA Wkmin (g).
We showed that, unless k is a collapsing level (i.e., Wkmin (g) “collapses” to its affine subal-
gebra), the VOA Wkmin (g) can be unitary only if the conjugate linear involution of g which
induces that of Wkmin (g) is “almost compact”, and then it is essentially unique. We went on
to classify in [15] the values of k for which the minimal simple W -algebras are unitary, and to
classify their unitary highest weight representations. In [17] we extended these results to the
Ramond sector. It turned out that different methods work for non-extremal and extremal
representations (physicists call them massive and massless, respectively [8, 11, 26]), which,
roughly speaking, means that they enter or do not enter in “continuous” families, respectively.
We refer to Section 2 for precise definitions and statements.
   The non-extremal unitary highest weight representations of minimal W -algebras were stud-
ied using the generalized Fairlie construction [14, 16], and the determinant of the Hermitian
form, which allowed us to completely and uniformly classify them. However there seems to
be no unified approach to the construction of massless unitary representations for minimal
W -algebras, though a unified conjectural list is given in [15]. In all known cases they are
constructed using a free field realization [8, 15, 26], or a coset construction [11].
   The main purpose of the present paper is to prove unitarity of the extremal highest weight
modules, both untwisted and Ramond twisted, over the big N = 4 superconformal algebra,
confirming our conjectures 2.3 and 2.5 and the results of [11] (of which we were unaware
when writing [15], [17]).
   It is well-known that for a compact Lie group G and its closed connected subgroup A
the coset construction of the N = 2 superconformal algebra is possible if the manifold G/A
carries an invariant integrable complex structure [23, 24]. According to the paper [27] of
Sevrin et al., there is a coset construction of the big N = 4 superconformal algebra if G/A
admits an invariant hypercomplex structure. A general construction of such a structure was
given in Joyce [12], and in Section 6 we recall this construction, following [4]. Applying this
construction in Section 7 to the coset SU (n)/SU (n − 2) we obtain in Section 8 a vertex

coset costruction produces all the massless unitary representations of the remaining minimal

is given in [15], and in [17] for the Ramond sector.
   Little is known about the classification of unitary highest weight representations of Wk (g, s)
where s is a non-minimal sl2 subalgebra of g. It is proved in [14] that these VOA are of strong
CFT type. It is also proved in [14, Remark 7.10] that for a non-collapsing level k, Wk (g, s)
can be unitary only if the grading of g defined by a non-zero semisimple element of s is
compatible with the parity of the superalgebra g. In the case when g is a Lie algebra this
means that a non-zero nilpotent element of s is even.

    2. Unitarity of highest weight representations of minimal W -algebras
    Let g be a simple finite-dimensional Lie superalgebra, over C, with a reductive even part
g0̄ and invariant non-degenerate bilinear form (·|·), with restriction to g0̄ non-degenerate. Let
s = Span{e, x, f }, where [e, f ] = x, [x, e] = e, [x, f ] = −f be an sl2 subalgebra of g0̄ . To the
datum (g, s, k ∈ C) one associates the universal quantum affine W -algebra W k (g, s) of level
k by the quantum Hamiltonian reduction [19], [21]. If k is different from the critical level
kcrit (which we assume), the vertex algebra W k (g, s) has a unique maximal ideal, and the
quotient by this ideal is a simple W -algebra, denoted by Wk (g, s). These VOA are of strong
CFT type [14].
    The minimal W -algebras Wmin  k (g) correspond to a choice of s, called minimal, for which

linear involution ϕ of the vertex algebra Wmin k (g) is necessarily induced by a conjugate linear

its affine part.) Moreover, it is proved in [15, Proposition 8.9] that the vertex algebra Wkmin (g)

Remark 10.6. Lemma 10.4 proves that the representations L−+ (0, r2 ) and L−+ (r1 , n − 2)

ϵ2 − ϵ3 (see [17, 10.5]). Thus Proposition 10.5 proves their unitarity.

        U + = span({Ej,1 | 2 ≤ j ≤ n − 1} ∪ {Ej,n | 2 ≤ j ≤ n − 1} ∪ {ϕ1 (h2 ), ϕ1 (e)}),
