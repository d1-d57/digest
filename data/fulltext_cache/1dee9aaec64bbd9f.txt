Tao, Rogers' theorem on sieving. Removing cosets from finite cyclic group: union of translated cosets >= union of original subgroups; sieved density maximized when residues vanish. Obscure result (only in 1966 Halberstam-Roth book, attributed to Rogers unpublished). Resolves Erdos Problem 281 via 1936 Davenport-Erdos result. Proof via total ordering of subgroups in prime-power cyclic groups + coprime products; compression intuition. Fails for non-cyclic groups.
