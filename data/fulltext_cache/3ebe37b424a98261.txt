Known facts (via search/Wikipedia/Copenhagen math dept summary): Bent Fuglede (1925-2023), Danish
mathematician, professor at University of Copenhagen / Technical University of Denmark, known for
Fuglede's theorem (in operator theory) and Fuglede's conjecture (spectral sets / tiling, a
famous open problem in harmonic analysis/geometry disproved in general by Tao in 2004 but active
research area since). Also contributed to potential theory, finely harmonic/holomorphic functions,
pluripotential theory, and moment problems. Founding editorial board member of Expositiones
Mathematicae since 1983, honorary member until his death.
