In the paper we study the Bott-Chern and Aeppli cohomologies of the twistor space of a compact self-dual 4-manifold and we characterize the validity of the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma. We also compute explicitly the Dolbeault cohomology of the twistor space Z Z of the flat 4 4 -dimensional torus, which is known to not satisfy the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} lemma. Introduction The Bott-Chern and Aeppli cohomology groups of a compact complex manifold X X : H B ​ C ∙ , ∙ ​ ( X ) := ker ​ ∂ ∩ ker ⁡ ∂ ¯ im ​ ∂ ∂ ¯ , H A ∙ , ∙ ​ ( X ) := ker ​ ∂ ∂ ¯ im ​ ∂ + im ​ ∂ ¯ H^{\bullet,\bullet}_{BC}(X):=\frac{\ker\partial\cap\ker\overline{\partial}}{\mathrm{im}\,\partial\overline{\partial}}\;,\qquad H^{\bullet,\bullet}_{A}(X):=\frac{\ker\partial\overline{\partial}}{\mathrm{im}\,\partial+\mathrm{im}\,\overline{\partial}} provide an important geometric and analytic information when X X does not admit any Kähler metric. For instance, in [ 2 ] it is shown that a compact complex manifold satisfies the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma if and only if its Bott-Chern cohomology satisfies a Frölicher-type equality. However, these spaces are very hard to compute and there are only few examples in which any information about them is available. In this paper we consider the case when X X is a twistor space of a compact self-dual 4-manifold. Twistor spaces were introduced by R. Penrose [ 24 ] in the 60s as a tool to transform solutions of some nonlinear differential equations in mathematical physics into holomorphic objects in complex geometry. The theory was formalized and generalized in [ 3 ] where the twistor space Z Z of a 4-dimensional oriented Riemannian manifold ( M , g ) (M,g) is the associated bundle to the principal bundle of orthonormal positive frames with fiber all complex structures in ℝ 4 {\mathbb{R}}^{4} compatible with the Euclidean scalar product. It has a fiber identified with 𝕊 2 \mathbb{S}^{2} and comes equipped with a tautological almost complex structure J J which is integrable precisely when ( M , g ) (M,g) is self-dual. As Hitchin showed in [ 16 ] the only twistor spaces admitting a Kähler metric are Z ≡ ℂ ​ P 3 Z\equiv\mathbb{C}P^{3} of 𝕊 4 \mathbb{S}^{4} and Z ≡ F ​ l 1 , 2 Z\equiv Fl_{1,2} of ℂ ​ P 2 \mathbb{C}P^{2} , where F ​ l 1 , 2 Fl_{1,2} is the flag manifold of ℂ 3 \mathbb{C}^{3} . This fact makes the general twistor spaces of a self-dual manifolds an appropriate set of examples of non-Kähler manifolds. We note here that in this case Z Z admits a balanced metric [ 20 , 21 ] , but only the Kähler ones admit an SKT structure [ 28 ] . The 𝕊 2 \mathbb{S}^{2} fibers of the projection Z → M Z\rightarrow M are rational curves and in [ 12 ] another almost complex structure J 2 J_{2} was considered, which has an opposite sign on these fibers and is never integrable. The authors of [ 12 ] showed that a harmonic map from a Riemann surface into M M has a lift to a J 2 J_{2} -holomorphic map into Z Z . The Betti numbers of twistor spaces could be computed by Lerray-Hirsch theorem and in [ 13 ] the Frölicher spectral sequence { E r } \{E_{r}\} was studied. It was proved that { E r } \{E_{r}\} always degenerates at most at the second step and a characterization is given for when it degenerates at the first one. There are, in fact, several examples where it fails to degenerate at the first step; in such cases, the Bott-Chern and Aeppli cohomology groups differ from the Dolbeault ones, and the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma does not hold. In this work, we study the Bott-Chern and Aeppli cohomologies of twistor spaces and characterize the validity of the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma in this context, for which no general results were previously available. We compute the Bott-Chern and Aeppli diamonds of a twistor space (Theorem 5 ), and we show that the twistor space Z Z of a compact self-dual four-manifold M M satisfies the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma if and only if h B ​ C 1 , 1 ​ ( Z ) + h A 1 , 1 ​ ( Z ) = 2 ​ ( b + ​ ( M ) + 1 ) and h B ​ C 1 , 2 ​ ( Z ) = b 1 ​ ( M ) h^{1,1}_{BC}(Z)+h^{1,1}_{A}(Z)=2(b_{+}(M)+1)\quad\text{and}\quad h^{1,2}_{BC}(Z)=b_{1}(M) (Theorem 8 ). As a consequence, we prove that if M M is a compact self-dual simply-connected four-manifold such that its twistor space Z Z satisfies the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma, then M M must be diffeomorphic to either the 4 4 -sphere or to a connected sum # k ​ ℂ ​ P 2 \#_{k}\mathbb{C}P^{2} for some k ≥ 1 k\geq 1 . Notably, each of these manifolds admits a self-dual metric whose corresponding twistor space satisfies the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma. Using results from [ 7 ] and [ 8 ] , we conclude that the twistor space Z Z of a compact self-dual simply-connected four-manifold M M satisfies the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma if and only if Z Z lies in Fujiki’s class 𝒞 \mathcal{C} , or equivalently, if Z Z is Moishezon. In Section 3, we also consider the case of twistor spaces Z Z of fake projective planes M M , showing that the Frölicher spectral sequence of Z Z does not degenerate at the first step; hence, in particular, Z Z does not satisfy the ∂ ∂ ¯ \partial\overline{\partial} -lemma. Finally, in the last section, we compute explicitly the Dolbeault cohomology of the twistor space Z Z associated to the flat four-dimensional torus. Acknowledgments.
