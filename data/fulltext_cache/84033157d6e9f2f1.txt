Plank theorems and their applications: a survey
William Verreault. Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society. Preprint: arXiv:2203.05540
(full text fetched, 154KB; published version BLMS 2026, 47pp, 8 figures).

Abstract: Plank problems concern the covering of convex bodies by planks in Euclidean space and are
related to famous open problems in convex geometry. In this survey, we introduce plank problems and
present surprising applications of plank theorems in various areas of mathematics.

Content: Opens with a toy problem (covering a circular table with planks), then covers Tarski's plank
problem (1930s), Thøger Bang's generalization/solution and Bang's lemma, Keith Ball's plank theorem for
Banach spaces, strengthenings, spherical/discrete variants, plank problems on complex Hilbert spaces
and L^p(mu) spaces, a recent polynomial approach via zeros of polynomials on the sphere, and
connections to analysis and number theory. Explicitly aims for "easy access to all readers," includes
historical background and proof details; author notes lack of any recent comprehensive survey on the
topic.
