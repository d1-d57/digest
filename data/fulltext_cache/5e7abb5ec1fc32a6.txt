Survey on the Farrell-Jones Conjecture
Wolfgang Lück. Bulletin of the AMS (New Series). Preprint: arXiv:2507.11337 (full text fetched, 287KB).

Abstract: This is a survey on the Farrell-Jones Conjecture about the algebraic K- and L-theory of
group rings and its applications to algebra, geometry, group theory, and topology.

Introduction (excerpt): "The Farrell-Jones Conjecture is devoted to the algebraic K- and L-theory of
the group ring RG of a group G and a ring R. Certainly K- and L-theory are rather sophisticated
theories. Group rings are very difficult rings... So studying the algebraic K-theory and L-theory of
group rings is hard and seems to be on the first glance a very special problem. So why should one
care?" — the survey motivates the conjecture for non-specialists before covering 36 pages of
applications spanning algebra (group rings, K/L-theory), geometry (manifolds, group actions), and
topology. Written by a leading expert in the field (Lück), comprehensive and well-organized survey of
known cases and applications.
