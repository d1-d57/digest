Abstract
A unifying framework for some extremal problems on locally compact Abelian groups is considered,
special cases of which include the Delsarte and Turán extremal problems. A slight variation of
the extremal problem is introduced and the different formulations are studied for equivalence.
Extending previous work, a general result on existence of extremal functions for the new variant
is proved under a certain general topological condition.
Keywords: Locally compact Abelian groups, positive definite functions, extremal problems,
Delsarte problem, Turán problem

The idea of studying this type of extremal problems in the general setting of LCA groups goes
back to [13]. However, we note that the extremal problem of maximising the L2 -norm was already
considered in the group setting in [7]. Subsequently, extremal problems of this type on LCA groups
have been studied in [16, 2, 10, 15]. Whereas [13, 16, 2] derive estimates for the extremal values and
relate them to packing in LCA groups, the theme in [10, 15] is to study the existence of an extremal
function. In [10], the existence of an extremal function is proved for a collection of band-limited
functions, i.e., functions whose Fourier transforms vanish outside some prescribed subset. In [15],
existence is proved without the restriction that the functions in the collection are band-limited,
generalising [5, Proposition A.1] to arbitrary LCA groups.
Although the result of [15] is quite general, the existence of an extremal function for the Turán
and Delsarte extremal problems was known before [15] for specific groups G and specific subsets
Ω+ . For instance, for the Turán problem in Rd it was known that if Ω+ is a closed convex subset
of Rd that tiles Rd by translations or when Ω+ is a closed ball in Rd , then an extremal function
exists (see [1, 12, 11]). On the other hand, if Ω+ is, for instance, an open convex set in Rd , an
extremal function does not exist.
