The equations and singularities
In 1757, Euler formulated a set of equations, now known as the

$=$

Especially for this reason, the equations remain a fascinating source of challenging mathematical problems connecting many branches of pure and applied mathematics, including PDE, analysis, geometry, and topology. There are also related fluid equations accounting for relativistic effects, the
relativistic Euler equations

Maximal globally hyperbolic developments
Due to finite speed of propagation for equations and
1

Here and throughout, classical solutions are defined to be smooth functions that solve the equation at every point at which they are defined. This naturally leads to the following question, of
fundamental mathematical and physical significance

What is the
