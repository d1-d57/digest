Isoperimetric inequalities in high-dimensional convex sets
Lecture notes by Bo'az Klartag and Joseph Lehec (Institut Henri Poincare, Paris, May 2024).
Bulletin of the AMS (New Series). Preprint: arXiv:2406.01324 (full text fetched via arxiv.org/html, 535KB).

Abstract: These are lecture notes focusing on recent progress towards Bourgain's slicing problem and
the isoperimetric conjecture proposed by Kannan, Lovasz and Simonovits (KLS).

Content: 81 pages of lecture notes prepared for an IHP summer school, targeting graduate students and
researchers. Starts from classical foundations (Poincare's inequality, log-concave measures) and builds
up through spherical/Gaussian concentration of measure, convex localization, optimal transport with
Monge cost, Bochner identities and curvature, heat flow, and Eldan's stochastic localization technique,
toward the recent resolution/near-resolution of Bourgain's slicing conjecture and the KLS conjecture.
Explicitly pedagogical: opens each section motivating the classical inequality before building to the
modern probabilistic/geometric machinery. Bridges functional analysis, probability theory, and convex
geometry.
