The author, JSE, published a review in the New York Times of a book about mathematics foundations by Jason Socrates Bardi ("The Great Math War"). The reviewer noted the work had interesting virtues but was also quite badly overwritten. The blog post includes an extended passage that didn't make the final published review, critiquing the book's mathematical clarity. Two examples: a lengthy metaphor about galaxies and sand buckets attempting to explain different infinities, which the author found hard to parse even with mathematical background; and a statement that "cardinality of an infinite set is like the Highlander – there can be only one!" which the reviewer labels as not, in fact, what the continuum hypothesis says, and calls false, since Cantor's theory establishes that infinite sets have many different cardinalities. The author concludes this explanation sacrifices clarity for a verbal flourish.
