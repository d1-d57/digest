Generalised Fermat equation: a survey of solved cases
Ashleigh Ratcliffe, Bogdan Grechuk. Expositiones Mathematicae 43 (2025).
Preprint: arXiv:2412.11933 (full text fetched, 388KB).

Abstract: Generalised Fermat equation (GFE) is the equation of the form ax^p+by^q=cz^r, where
a,b,c,p,q,r are positive integers. If 1/p+1/q+1/r<1, GFE is known to have at most finitely many
primitive integer solutions (x,y,z). A large body of the literature is devoted to finding such
solutions explicitly for various six-tuples (a,b,c,p,q,r), as well as for infinite families of such
six-tuples. This paper surveys the families of parameters for which GFE has been solved. Although the
proofs are not discussed here, collecting these references in one place will make it easier for the
readers to find the relevant proof techniques in the original papers. Also, this survey will help the
readers to avoid duplicate work by solving the already solved cases.

Explicitly a reference/bibliography compilation rather than an expository proof-walkthrough: "the
proofs are not discussed here." Useful practical resource for the number theory community rather than
a broad accessibility bridge.
