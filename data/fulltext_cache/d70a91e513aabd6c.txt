Hölder regularity of stable solutions to elliptic equations up to R^9: full quantitative proofs
Xavier Cabré. Bulletin of the AMS (New Series). Preprint: arXiv:2205.11352 (full text fetched, 616KB).

Abstract: This article concerns the results obtained in [Cabré, Figalli, Ros-Oton, and Serra, Acta
Math. 224 (2020)], which established the Hölder regularity of stable solutions to semilinear elliptic
equations in the optimal range of dimensions n<=9. For expository purposes, we provide self-contained
proofs of all results. They involve only basic Analysis tools and are intended to be accessible to a
broader mathematical audience beyond PDE specialists. Two of the results in the 2020 article relied on
compactness arguments. Here we present, instead, quantitative proofs from the more recent paper [Cabré,
to appear in Amer. J. Math, arXiv:2211.13033]. They allow to quantify the Hölder regularity exponent
and simplify significantly the treatment of boundary regularity. We also comment on similar progress
and open problems for related equations.

Introduction: traces the history of the regularity problem for stable solutions to -Delta u = f(u),
motivated by Gelfand's combustion problem, from Joseph-Lundgren's 1970s work onward, up to the 2020
resolution in the optimal dimension range n<=9. Explicitly self-declared expository article aimed at
making a landmark PDE regularity theorem accessible outside the PDE specialist community, dedicated
"In memory of Haim Brezis and Ireneo Peral."
