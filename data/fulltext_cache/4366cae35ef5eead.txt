72,000 different notions of the center of a triangle
.  I knew the incenter, the circumcenter, and the centroid.  Those are indeed the first three.  The fourth is the orthocenter.  I guess, when I think about it, I knew the three altitudes were concurrent so I knew this existed.  The fifth is the
nine-point center
, which I’m pretty sure I’ve never heard of.  It’s the midpoint between the circumcenter and the orthocenter, though.  There are 70,000 more or so.  I am happy this exists.
