Interview with Sir John Kingman
Ana Isabel Mendes and Martin Raussen




Sir John Kingman, Fellow of the Royal Society, was the 4th president
of the European Mathematical Society during the years 2003–2006.
From his career as a professor of probability theory and statistics
to holding top positions as a science administrator and leader, his
professional journey continued until his retirement in 2006. Now,
at age 86, he does not do contemporary mathematics any longer,
but he still likes to think about and solve non-trivial puzzles of
a mathematical flavour.
     In January 2025, Ana Mendes and Martin Raussen had the
opportunity to ask Sir John about his life within and around math-
ematics. Among many other things, we learned that mathematics
is a fuzzy and messy subject, nevertheless connected, and quite
often very useful!
