Richard Rusczyk received a $5 million grant from the Bezos Courage and Civility Award. The blogger, who knows Rusczyk from high school Math Olympiad training, celebrates this recognition of the Art of Problem Solving (AOPS) platform. The blogger praises AOPS for emphasizing "depth over acceleration," explaining that students spend a full year mastering algebra rather than rushing through material. This approach reflects the belief that meaningful mathematical learning should be "deep, richly, broadly, and well" rather than focused on advancing to higher course numbers quickly. The blogger appreciates AOPS's text-only format, stating that "math is made of words" and that this monomedia approach makes pedagogical sense. A personal anecdote reveals that Rusczyk, from Alabama, introduced the blogger to the phrase "my bad" during their 1988 Math Olympiad training.
