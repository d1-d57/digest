Review: Analytic Number Theory Revealed: A First Guide to Prime Numbers (Andrew Granville)
MAA Reviews, reviewer Michael Berg, published 2026-06-19
URL: https://maa.org/book-reviews/analytic-number-theory-revealed-a-first-guide-to-prime-numbers/

Overview: Berg reviews Andrew Granville's "Analytic Number Theory Revealed: A First Guide to Prime Numbers," published by the American Mathematical Society in October 2025. The 292-page textbook is priced at $79.00.

Background and Context: This volume follows Granville's earlier works, "Number Theory Revealed: An Introduction" and the expanded "Number Theory Revealed: A Masterclass." Berg notes the predecessor covers "solving the general cubic, followed by solving the general quartic; the dynamics of the Euclidean Algorithm" and other topics, comparing its breadth to Hardy and Wright's classic 1938 text.

Content Coverage: The current book addresses sieve methods, the Prime Number Theorem, Dirichlet's Theorem on arithmetic progressions, and 17 additional independent topics. These include "Selberg's small sieve, primes in short intervals, primes missing digits," and the Hardy-Littlewood circle method.

Pedagogical Approach: Berg praises Granville's "compact, clear, and thorough" presentation, noting extensive historical context and biographical sketches of mathematicians including Gauss, Ramanujan, and Riemann.

Assessment: The reviewer enthusiastically recommends the book for advanced undergraduates and graduate students, stating he "can't recommend it highly enough," while noting even experienced researchers will find value in its presentation.
