Tao et al, Mathematical exploration and discovery at scale. AlphaEvolve LLM-powered code evolution applied to 67 problems in analysis, combinatorics, geometry. Rediscovered Gerver sofa, improved Kakeya/Nikodym/Sidon set bounds, exact Talenti functions for Gagliardo-Nirenberg. Weaknesses: verifier exploitation, training data leakage, no breakthroughs on Sidorenko/Sendov/Crouzeix conjectures. Emphasizes human role in problem formulation and verification.
