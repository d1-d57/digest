Gowers, Creating a database of motivated proofs. Proposes structured database of proofs built from documented 'standard moves' (library extraction, metavariable instantiation, generalization/specialization) rather than polished but opaque conventional proofs, to help train/guide LLMs beyond guess-and-check. Funded by Renaissance Philanthropies' $9M AI for Math Fund. Point-and-click interface prototype exists; seeking proof submissions (target 1000 in 2 years), developers, beta testers, moderators.
