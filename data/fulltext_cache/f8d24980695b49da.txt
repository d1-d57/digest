Quanta Books, an independent subsidiary of the Simons Foundation, announced two forthcoming popular
science books:

1. "Everything Is Fields" by David Tong (theoretical physicist, University of Cambridge) — explores
quantum field theory for general audiences; per Tong, the book "reveals the hidden unity that ties
together particles and forces," presenting the idea that all matter, light, and even human bodies
consist of waves oscillating across quantum fields permeating the universe.

2. "Six Math Essentials" by Terence Tao (Fields Medalist, UCLA) — his first popular mathematics book,
examining six foundational concepts: numbers, algebra, geometry, probability, analysis, and dynamics.
Tao: "mathematics has become unnecessarily intimidating and abstruse to the general public while
being more essential than ever in the modern world." Due in US bookstores November 2026, with
international translations planned.

Publisher Thomas Lin calls the authors "intellectual powerhouses and seasoned communicators." The
imprint (with Farrar, Straus and Giroux) publishes works illuminating modern science's central
questions through storytelling. Debut title: "The Proof in the Code" by Kevin Hartnett, June 2026.
