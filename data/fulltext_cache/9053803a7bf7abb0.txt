A remark on some punctual Quot schemes on smooth projective curves
Atsushi Ito. EMS Surveys in Mathematical Sciences. Preprint: arXiv:2504.01335 (full text fetched, 178KB).

Abstract: For a locally free sheaf E on a smooth projective curve, we can define the punctual Quot
scheme which parametrizes torsion quotients of E of length n supported at a fixed point. It is known
that the punctual Quot scheme is a normal projective variety with canonical Gorenstein singularities.
In this note, we show that the punctual Quot scheme is a Q-factorial Fano variety of Picard number one.

NOTE: Despite appearing in "EMS Surveys in Mathematical Sciences" (the journal name), this is a short
(9-page) research note proving one specific technical property, not a survey/expository article. No
motivation for broader audiences; narrow algebraic-geometry technical result.
