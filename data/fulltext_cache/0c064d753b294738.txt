The notion of weak tiling was a key ingredient in the proof of Fuglede’s spectral set conjecture for convex bodies, due to the fact that every spectral set tiles its complement weakly with a suitable Borel measure. In this paper we review the concept of weak tiling, and answer a question raised by Kolountzakis, Lev and Matolcsi, by giving an example of a set T 𝑇 T italic_T which tiles its complement weakly, but T 𝑇 T italic_T is neither spectral, nor a proper tile.
